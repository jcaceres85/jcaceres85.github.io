📌 About Me

José David Cáceres Coello is an Environmental Engineer with a Master’s degree in Geographical Information Technologies and more than 15 years of professional and research experience in territorial management, remote sensing, geographic information systems (GIS), climate change, and disaster risk management.

He currently serves as Profesor Titular III at the Faculty of Spatial Sciences of the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de Honduras (UNAH) and as Coordinator of the Observatorio Universitario de Ordenamiento Territorial (OUOT). His work includes academic teaching, research, geospatial analysis, project development, and the design of geospatial platforms for territorial analysis and risk management.

🚀 Main Projects
1. Geoportal OUOT

🔗 https://geoportalouot.unah.edu.hn/

A geospatial visualization and analysis platform supporting territorial management and decision-making, developed under the Observatorio Universitario de Ordenamiento Territorial.

2. WebODM UNAH

🔗 https://webodm.unah.edu.hn/

Institutional photogrammetry platform for drone image processing, enabling the generation of orthophotos, DSMs, and other geospatial products.

3. Territorios en Riesgo

🔗 https://territoriosenriesgo.unah.edu.hn/

A specialized portal for the analysis, monitoring, and visualization of disaster risk scenarios in Honduras.

4. Kobo UNAH

🔗 https://kobo.unah.edu.hn/

Platform for digital data collection using KoboToolbox, supporting field surveys and mobile data capture workflows for academic and research purposes.

5. SIROS (Sistema de Información para el Reporte de Observaciones y Seguimiento)

🔗 https://siros.unah.edu.hn/

System for reporting territorial observations, monitoring field activities, and supporting data-driven decision-making.
